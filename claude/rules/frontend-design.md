# Aesthetic Direction (frontend-design)

Optional add-on to the Design Brief in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` section 2, for setups with the `frontend-design` skill (and, for non-product media, `theme-factory`, `web-artifacts-builder`, `canvas-design`). In the brief's precedence order it ranks below the brand reference and `ui-ux-pro-max`.

- **Aesthetic Direction & Component Craft**: use `frontend-design` for the step that settles whatever is still open into the compact brief.
- **Other media**: use `theme-factory`, `web-artifacts-builder` or `canvas-design` only when the target medium is an artifact, slide or static image.
- **Hand-off**: `agy`'s local `frontend-design` skill is for what the brief leaves open, and anything taken from it goes under `DECISIONS`.
